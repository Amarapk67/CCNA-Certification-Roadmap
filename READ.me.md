## Weekly study routine (10--12 hours)

-   [ ] **Session 1 --- 2 hours:** Learn concepts and review exam
    objectives.
-   [ ] **Session 2 --- 3 hours:** Build the week's Packet Tracer lab
    from scratch.
-   [ ] **Session 3 --- 3 hours:** Troubleshoot intentional faults and
    verify with `show` commands, `ping`, and `traceroute`.
-   [ ] **Session 4 --- 2--4 hours:** Practice questions, subnetting
    drills, command recall, and error-log review.

## Milestone checklist

-   [ ] **October 23 --- Foundations complete:** Subnetting, IPv4/IPv6,
    and basic connectivity.
-   [ ] **November 20 --- Switching complete:** VLANs, trunks, STP,
    EtherChannel, and troubleshooting.
-   [ ] **December 11 --- Routing and services complete:** Static
    routes, OSPF, DHCP, NAT, and ACLs.
-   [ ] **January 8 --- Blueprint covered:** Every official v1.1
    objective reviewed at least once.
-   [ ] **January 22 --- Exam readiness:** Aim for 85%+ on reputable
    timed practice exams and no major weak domain.
-   [ ] **January 30 --- Target exam date:** Sit CCNA 200-301 v1.1, if
    available and ready.

## Exam booking checklist

-   [ ] Check Cisco Certification Tracking System and Pearson VUE
    access.
-   [ ] Review test-center and online-proctored appointment options.
-   [ ] By **December 4**, try to reserve a January 30 appointment.
-   [ ] Confirm the booking explicitly says **CCNA 200-301 v1.1** before
    paying.
-   [ ] Confirm exam version, price, ID requirements, time zone, and
    cancellation/rescheduling rules.
-   [ ] Reconfirm appointment details one week before the exam.

## Exam version transition

-   **Target:** Complete the v1.1 exam before the transition deadline.
-   **Expected transition:** v2.0 begins February 3, 2027; v1.1 testing
    is expected to end February 2, 2027.
-   **If the deadline is missed:** Check Cisco's current exam page and
    use the v2.0 blueprint to identify changes before continuing
    preparation.

Always verify dates and exam-version availability on the official Cisco
and Pearson VUE websites, as scheduling and transition details can
change.
